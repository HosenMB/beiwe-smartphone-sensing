# Beiwe App — Setup Instructions (iPhone & Android)

Thank you for participating in this study! We chose this mobile app for data
collection because it lets us collect short surveys at scheduled times during
the day. Please follow the steps for **your** phone below.

---

## iPhone (iOS)

1. **Download the app.** Open the **App Store** and search for **`beiwe2`**.
   Download **Beiwe2** (developer: *Onnela Lab LLC*, category: *Education*).
2. **Open the app.** You'll see a Welcome page, then the **Register for Study**
   page.
3. **Fill in the Register form** using *Registration Info* above, then tap
   **Register**.
4. **You're set.** You'll land on the **Study** page ("There are no active
   surveys to take at this time"). Surveys will notify you and appear here at
   their scheduled times.

---

## Android

Android does not use the Play Store — you download the app directly from the
study website.

1. **Go to the login page.** Open the study link in your browser:
   `https://beiwe9.hif-umbc.com/`. You'll see a **Log In** page.
2. **Find the download link.** At the bottom-right it says
   *"Looking for the Android App?"* — tap **click here** to download the Beiwe
   app from the site.
3. **Install it.** You'll be prompted to install the app. Because it's being
   installed from the internet (not the Play Store), you may see a
   **Google Play Protect** warning like *"Harmful app blocked — This app may be
   harmful."* This is expected. Choose the option to **install anyway** and
   finish downloading the app.
4. **Open the app.** You'll see a Welcome page, then the **Register for Study**
   page.
5. **Fill in the Register form** using *Registration Info* above, then tap
   **Register**.
6. **You're set.** You'll land on the **Study** page ("There are no active
   surveys to take at this time"). Surveys will notify you and appear here at
   their scheduled times.
